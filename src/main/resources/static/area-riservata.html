This account

Change password Every session is signed out afterwards, this one included. A password change that leaves old sessions alive has not actually revoked anything.

Sign out everywhere Deletes every session row for this account, not only this browser's. The one to reach for when a laptop is out of your hands.
Start the course again Clears the server's copy of your chapter and card progress. Your notes are kept, and this browser's own local copy is left alone — it will re-upload on the next sync unless you clear it in the course as well.
Delete this account Account, sessions, progress, study days and notes, in one transaction. There is no undo and no backup. Type your username to confirm.
